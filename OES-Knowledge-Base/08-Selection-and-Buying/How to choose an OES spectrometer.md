# How to choose an OES spectrometer

A decision guide. Work top-down: **what you analyse** drives **the optics and detector**, which
drive **the budget**.

## 1. Define the job

- **Matrices** — which base metals? (Fe only? + Al/Cu/Ni?) Each adds calibrations & CRMs.
  → [[MOC - Applications]]
- **Elements & levels** — need **carbon / low ppm light elements**? Then you need
  [[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed optics|vacuum/UV optics]]. Trace work → low
  [[Detection limits, precision and accuracy|detection limits]].
- **Grades / standards** — certifying to ASTM/EN/GB grades? → [[MOC - Standards]].
- **Throughput** — samples/day, melt-control time pressure → [[Foundry melt control workflow]].
- **Location** — clean lab vs foundry deck vs field → [[Stationary vs mobile vs portable OES]].

## 2. Match the hardware

| Requirement | Points you toward |
|---|---|
| Highest resolution, high-alloy/superalloys | **750 mm**, PMT — [[JB-750]] |
| Broad VUV element coverage (C,P,S,N) | **Vacuum optics** — [[Innovate T5]], [[JB-750]] |
| Flexible multi-matrix, add lines in software | **CMOS array** — [[Innovate T5]], [[Noble T7]] |
| Research/cleanliness, top accuracy class | Constant-temp CMOS — [[Noble T7]] |
| Compact / lower-upkeep lab, entry-mid | **Sealed optics** — [[Exquis T4]] |
| On-site / large parts that can't be cut | **Mobile OES** — [[Surpass F1]] |
| Carbon can't be measured; just sort heavy elements fast | **XRF** — [[F6000 Pro]] |

Background: [[Detectors - PMT vs CCD-CMOS]] · [[Diffraction gratings and focal length]] ·
[[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed optics]].

## 3. Look past the headline spec

- **Wavelength range & resolution** (covers your lines without overlap?).
- **CRM / calibration package** for *your* grades — often the real cost driver
  ([[Calibration and certified reference materials]]).
- **Consumables & cost of ownership** → [[Total cost of ownership and consumables]].
- **Software**: corrections, SPC, charge-correction, grade libraries, single-spark
  ([[Single spark evaluation (PDA-SDA)]]).
- **Service, training, warranty, installation, argon/utilities** at your site.
- **Sample-prep equipment** budget — the result depends on it ([[Sample preparation overview]]).

## 4. Beware the spec-sheet traps
"Number of elements" and "ppm detection limits" are matrix/line-specific marketing unless tied to
*your* element list. Ask for **demonstrated precision on your CRMs** and a **sample-analysis
trial**.
