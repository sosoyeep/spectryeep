# Total cost of ownership and consumables

The purchase price is the start. Budget the **running costs** — they often dominate over the
instrument's life.

## Recurring consumables

- **Argon** — continuous during analysis and purge; high-purity grade for UV work
  ([[Argon supply and purity]]). Often the **largest ongoing cost**; sealed-optics units
  ([[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed optics]]) cut purge gas.
- **Counter-electrodes** (tungsten) and **spark-stand parts/windows**
  ([[Spark stand and counter electrode]]).
- **Abrasive belts/discs & cutter inserts** for [[Surface grinding and milling|sample prep]] —
  dedicated per matrix.
- **Certified reference materials** — calibration + recal + control samples; they **expire** and
  must be replenished; per-grade coverage drives cost
  ([[Calibration and certified reference materials]]).
- For combustion/fusion analysers: **crucibles, accelerators, reagents, furnace cleaning**
  ([[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis]], [[ONH inert gas fusion analysis]]).

## Periodic / fixed

- **Vacuum pump** service (vacuum optics); **optics profiling**; PMT/CMOS health.
- **Service contract**, calibration verification, software updates, operator **training**.
- **Utilities & environment** — stable power, temperature, clean dry air; some units need
  cooling/AC ([[Optical system - Paschen-Runge and Rowland circle]]).
- **Downtime cost** — a melt deck waiting on a down OES is expensive; weigh **uptime/service**
  heavily.

## Cost levers
- **Sealed optics** → lowest gas + no vacuum pump ([[Exquis T4]]); **vacuum** → best
  performance, pump upkeep; **PMT** → fixed channels (no surprise add-on hardware) but per-line
  cost up front.
- **Auto-cleaning** features reduce labour/downtime (e.g. [[CS2020]]'s auto dual-brush + vacuum
  dust removal → 200+ maintenance-free cycles).
- Right-size the **CRM set** to grades you actually certify.
