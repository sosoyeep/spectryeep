# Noble T7 OES Spectrometer

> Research-grade CMOS spark OES with a **constant-temperature optical chamber** and an
> **accuracy class around <10 ppm** — JIEBO's high-accuracy unit.

## Highlights
- **±0.1 °C thermal stabilisation** of the optics → very low drift, less frequent
  [[Type standardization and drift correction|standardisation]].
- **Wide 120–800 nm** range → more analytical lines, fewer
  [[Spectral interferences and matrix effects|interferences]] options.
- Suited to **cleanliness / research** work with [[Single spark evaluation (PDA-SDA)]].

## Best for
Research labs and demanding QC chasing the lowest detection limits and best repeatability;
sub-10-ppm work. See [[Nickel cobalt and superalloys]], [[How to choose an OES spectrometer]].
