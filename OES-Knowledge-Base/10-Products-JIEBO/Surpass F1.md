# Surpass F1 Mobile OES Spectrometer

> Cart-mounted **mobile spark OES** with built-in battery and argon — brings **carbon and UV
> light elements to the part** when it can't come to the lab (45 kg with battery).

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |

| Optical system | [[Optical system - Paschen-Runge and Rowland circle\|Paschen–Runge]], dual grating |

## Highlights
- **True mobile spark OES** — keeps a UV optic + argon, so unlike most handhelds it measures
  **carbon and the VUV light elements** on-site ([[OES vs XRF vs LIBS]]).
- **Built-in lithium battery + argon cylinder** on a trolley → fully portable to large/fixed
  parts; **jet electrode** for spotting.
- Dual-grating UV+visible for resolution where it matters.

## Best for
PMI and composition checks on **pipelines, pressure vessels, large castings, structures, rebar**
— anywhere carbon matters but the part can't be cut. See
[[Stationary vs mobile vs portable OES]].
