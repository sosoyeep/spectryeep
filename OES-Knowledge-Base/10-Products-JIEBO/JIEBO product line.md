# JIEBO product line

**Wuxi Jiebo Instrument Technology Co., Ltd.** (brand: **JIEBO Instrument**; founded 2009, Wuxi,
Jiangsu, China) manufactures elemental-analysis instruments for metals: **spark OES**,
**high-frequency infrared C/S**, **inert-gas-fusion O/N/H**, and **handheld XRF/LIBS**.
Philosophy: *"International Quality, Domestic Price, Personal Service."*

> [!info] Specs in these notes are from the **JIEBO 2026 brochure** (`SOURCE_FROM_BROCHURE.md`)
> and `src/consts.ts`. Verify against current datasheets before quoting — see
> [[Sources and further reading]].

### 2. Combustion & fusion analysers
- [[CS2020]] — high-frequency IR **carbon & sulfur**, 4 cells, auto-clean
- [[CS995]] — bench C/S
- [[CS996]] — floor-standing C/S, wider range
- [[ONH-508]] — **oxygen / nitrogen / hydrogen** (inert-gas fusion)

### 3. Handheld
- [[F6000 Pro]] — handheld **XRF** (Ti–U)
- [[F7000 Pro]] — handheld **LIBS** (light elements, fast)

## Target industries
Foundry & casting, steel mills/metallurgy, aerospace, automotive, machinery, scrap recycling,
research, nuclear, mining, new-energy materials.
