# CS996 Carbon & Sulfur Analyzer

> **Floor-standing** high-frequency infrared C/S analyser with a **wider range** than the
> [[CS995]] and expandable cells. Background: [[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis]].

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Floor / operating-table HF-IR carbon & sulfur analyser |
| Scope | C 0.0001–10.0000 % / S 0.0001–2.0000 % (wider than CS995) |
| Furnace power | 2.5 kW |
| Detection cells | 1 carbon + 1 sulfur (expandable low-C / high-S) |
| Dimensions | 1360 × 610 × 770 mm and 680 × 510 × 1260 mm |
| Weight | 215 kg |

## Highlights
- **Wider C/S range** and **expandable cells** for low-carbon or high-sulfur extremes.
- Floor-standing layout for higher-throughput labs.
- Matrix-independent total C/S reference for steel, iron, alloys.

## Best for
Labs needing broader C/S range / higher throughput than [[CS995]] but not the 4-cell auto-clean
of [[CS2020]].
