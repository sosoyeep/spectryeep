> Vacuum-chamber, all-digital, full-spectrum CMOS spark OES — JIEBO's **multi-matrix lab

## Highlights
- **Vacuum optics** reach the [[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed optics|VUV light elements]] (C, P, S,
  N) with low [[Detection limits, precision and accuracy|detection limits]].
- **All-digital excitation** → stable, multi-mode, supports [[Single spark evaluation (PDA-SDA)]].
- **CMOS full spectrum** → add lines/elements in software across 10 matrices.
