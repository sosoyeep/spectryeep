# Nickel, cobalt and superalloys

The most demanding common OES matrices: **Ni-base and Co-base superalloys** (Inconel, Hastelloy,
Monel, Nimonic, Stellite, Waspaloy …) for aerospace, energy and chemical-process service.

## Why they're demanding

- **Highly alloyed**: Cr, Co, Mo, W, Nb, Ta, Ti, Al, Fe, Mn, Si, plus trace controls →
  crowded UV spectra and severe [[Spectral interferences and matrix effects|line overlaps]].
  Rewards **long focal length / high resolution** ([[Diffraction gratings and focal length]],
  [[JB-750]]) and excellent [[Calibration and certified reference materials|matrix-matched
  CRMs]].
- **Tight trace limits** (e.g. low S, P, Pb, Bi, Ag, Se for hot-cracking control) → low
  [[Detection limits, precision and accuracy|detection limits]] and [[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed
  optics|vacuum/UV optics]].
- **Segregation / inclusions** matter → [[Single spark evaluation (PDA-SDA)|single-spark
  evaluation]] for cleanliness.

## What's measured
Ni/Co (matrix), Cr, Mo, W, Nb, Ta, Ti, Al, Fe, Mn, Si, C, B, Zr, Cu, V, Co/Ni, plus trace
S, P, Pb, Bi, Sn, As, Sb, Ag, Se, Te.

## Practice
- Grind ([[Surface grinding and milling]]); demanding samples → more burns, single-spark stats.
- Dedicated **per-family programs** (Ni-Cr-Fe vs Ni-Mo vs Co-base) with certified superalloy
  CRMs (IARM/Brammer, BAM, etc.).
- Standard: [[ASTM methods for spark OES|ASTM E3047 (nickel alloys)]] and related; verify edition.
- Confirm **O/N** with [[ONH inert gas fusion analysis|inert-gas fusion]] and **C/S** with
  [[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis|combustion]] for arbitration.

## JIEBO fit
Ni, Co, Ti are listed matrices for [[Innovate T5]], [[Noble T7]] and [[JB-750]]; superalloy
certification is the case for the **750 mm PMT** [[JB-750]].
