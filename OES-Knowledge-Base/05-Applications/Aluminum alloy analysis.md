# Aluminum alloy analysis

Aluminium is the second-biggest OES matrix after iron — wrought and cast alloys, primary and
secondary (recycled) metal.

## What's measured

Si, Fe, Cu, Mn, Mg, Zn, Ni, Ti, Cr, Pb, Sn, V, Zr, B, Be, Sr, Na, Ca, P …

- **Si** is the headline alloying element (e.g. Al-Si casting alloys); Mg, Cu, Zn define wrought
  series (2xxx, 5xxx, 6xxx, 7xxx).
- **Sr / Na** modification and **Ti/B** grain refinement are measured at low levels → benefit
  from good [[Detection limits, precision and accuracy|detection limits]] and
  [[Vacuum vs purged vs sealed optics|UV optics]].
- **Trace P** (affects modification) and tramp elements matter in **secondary/recycled** alloys.

## Sample preparation — DON'T grind

> [!important] Aluminium is soft: **mill or lathe** a clean face — grinding **smears** the metal
> and **embeds abrasive grit** (false Si/Al). Analyse **immediately**: a fresh Al surface
> re-oxidises within minutes. See [[Surface grinding and milling]] and
> [[Sample requirements by matrix]].

For melt samples, pour a **chill/disc mould** sample for a sound, homogeneous button; ensure full
solidification without porosity at the burn spot.

## Programs / sub-matrices
Separate calibrations for **cast (Al-Si)** vs **wrought** families, and often for **high-Si** vs
low-Si, because [[Spectral interferences and matrix effects|matrix effects]] differ. Use
Al-matrix CRMs ([[Calibration and certified reference materials]]). Standard:
[[ASTM methods for spark OES|ASTM E1251]]; [[Chinese GB-T standards|GB/T 7999]].

## Where it's used
Die-casting and foundry **melt control**, secondary-aluminium **furnace control**, extrusion
billet certification, automotive QC. → [[Foundry melt control workflow]].

## JIEBO fit
Al is a listed matrix for [[Innovate T5]], [[Noble T7]], [[Exquis T4]] (Fe/Al/Cu/Zn) and
[[JB-750]].
