# Copper alloy analysis

Coppers and copper alloys — **brasses** (Cu-Zn), **bronzes** (Cu-Sn, Cu-Al), **gunmetals**,
**cupronickels** (Cu-Ni), and high-conductivity coppers.

## What's measured

Zn, Sn, Pb, Ni, Fe, Mn, Al, Si, P, As, Sb, Bi, Ag, S, Be, Cr …

- **Zn** (brass), **Sn** (bronze), **Pb** (free-machining grades), **Ni** (cupronickel) define
  the families.
- **P** (deoxidised copper), **Ag** and **trace** As/Sb/Bi affect conductivity and hot-working.

## Sample preparation

Like aluminium, copper is relatively soft → **mill or lathe** the face rather than grind, to
avoid smearing and embedded grit ([[Surface grinding and milling]]).

> [!note] **Leaded** brasses/bronzes: Pb is nearly insoluble and **segregates** into globules,
> so single-burn Pb scatters. Average **more burns** and consider
> [[Single spark evaluation (PDA-SDA)|single-spark evaluation]] to read the lead reliably.

## Programs / sub-matrices
Distinct calibrations for **brass**, **tin/aluminium bronze**, **cupronickel**, **pure copper**,
etc., each with Cu-matrix CRMs. High Zn or Sn shifts
[[Spectral interferences and matrix effects|matrix behaviour]], so don't stretch one curve across
families.

## Where it's used
Copper/brass **foundries** (melt control), wrought mill **certification**, plumbing/electrical/
marine components, recycling/sorting of mixed copper scrap.

## JIEBO fit
Cu is a listed matrix for [[Innovate T5]], [[Noble T7]], [[Exquis T4]] and [[JB-750]].
