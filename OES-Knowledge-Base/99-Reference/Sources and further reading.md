# Sources and further reading

Where to verify and go deeper. **Always check the current edition** of any standard.

## Standards bodies (test methods & product specs)

- **ASTM International** — astm.org (E-series test methods → [[ASTM methods for spark OES]]).
- **ISO** — iso.org; **CEN/EN** — cencenelec.eu (→ [[ISO and EN methods]]).
- **SAC / China standards** (GB, GB/T) — national catalogue (→ [[Chinese GB-T standards]]).
- **JIS** (Japan), **DIN/BS** (national), **SAE/AMS** (aerospace).

## Reference data & RM providers

- **NIST Atomic Spectra Database (ASD)** — authoritative wavelengths/energy levels
  (→ [[Key spectral lines (analytical wavelengths)]]).
- **CRM producers:** NIST SRM, BAM, ECRM/EURONORM, IARM/Brammer Standard, BCS/BAS, JSS, China
  GBW/GSB (→ [[Calibration and certified reference materials]]).

## Background reading (concepts)

- General analytical-atomic-spectrometry texts (e.g. *Analytical Atomic Spectrometry* /
  spark-source spectrochemical analysis monographs) for the physics in
  [[MOC - Fundamentals]].
- OES instrument-maker application notes & handbooks (foundry, steel, aluminium) for matrix
  practice in [[MOC - Applications]].
- Metals-handbook / ASM references for grade composition limits.

## In-vault provenance

- Product specifications (the [[JIEBO product line]] notes) are from the **JIEBO 2026 product
  brochure** as captured in the parent repository (`SOURCE_FROM_BROCHURE.md`) and company data

  (`src/data/learn-*.ts`) and blog posts (`src/data/blog/*.ts`).
