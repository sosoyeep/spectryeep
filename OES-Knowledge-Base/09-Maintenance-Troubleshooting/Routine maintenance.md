# Routine maintenance

Preventive care keeps OES results trustworthy and uptime high. Adapt intervals to usage and the
maker's manual; let your [[Quality control and SPC|control charts]] tell you when to act sooner.

## Daily / each shift

- **Warm-up** the instrument and source before first analysis.
- **Profile** (wavelength align) if due, then **type-standardise**
  ([[Type standardization and drift correction]]).
- Run a **control sample**; record on the chart ([[Quality control and SPC]]).
- **Clean the [[Spark stand and counter electrode|spark stand]]**: brush/clean the
  counter-electrode, check/reset the gap, wipe the stand.
- Check **[[Argon supply and purity|argon]]** pressure/flow; don't run a cylinder near empty.

## Weekly / periodic

- **Vacuum out spark-stand dust** and exhaust path; empty traps.
- Inspect/replace **spark-stand window/disc**, electrodes, adapters.
- Replace worn **abrasive belts/cutters**; keep them matrix-dedicated.
- Check **leaks** on argon lines/fittings.
- Vacuum-optics: confirm the **vacuum level**; service the **pump** per schedule.

## Periodic / annual (often service-engineer)

- **Optics profiling & full re-standardisation**, lamp/source checks.
- **PMT/CMOS** health, electronics, cooling/temperature control verification
  ([[Optical system - Paschen-Runge and Rowland circle]]).
- Full **recalibration** if drift can no longer be corrected by standardisation.
- Review CRM stock & **expiry**; update control limits.

## Records
Keep a maintenance log alongside calibration/QC records — required for
[[Standards overview|17025-style]] traceability and invaluable for
[[Troubleshooting common problems|troubleshooting]].
