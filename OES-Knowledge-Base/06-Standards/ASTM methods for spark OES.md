# ASTM methods for spark OES

The most widely cited spark-AES / OES **test-method** standards. ASTM E01 committee (Analytical
Chemistry for Metals). **Confirm the current edition** ([[Standards overview]]).

## Spark atomic emission spectrometry — by matrix

| Standard | Scope (spark AES analysis of …) |
|---|---|
| **ASTM E415** | **Carbon and low-alloy steel** — the foundational method |
| **ASTM E1086** | **Austenitic stainless steel** |
| **ASTM E1999** | **Cast iron** |
| **ASTM E1251** | **Aluminium and aluminium alloys** |
| **ASTM E3047** | **Nickel alloys** |
| **ASTM E2209** | (high-temperature / Co-base & related — verify scope/edition) |

(Copper-alloy spark-OES exists under ASTM E-series as well; verify the current designation for
your grade family.)

## Practice & supporting standards

- **ASTM E1329** — *Verification and use of control charts in spectrochemical analysis*
  (underpins [[Quality control and SPC]]).
- **ASTM E305 / E1763 / E1601** — performance, interlaboratory study, and method-validation
  practices for optical-emission spectrometry (verify designations).
- **ASTM E135** — terminology for analytical chemistry of metals (a [[Glossary|glossary]]
  source).

## Combustion & fusion (complementary to OES)

| Standard | Scope |
|---|---|
| **ASTM E1019** | **C, S, N, O, and H** in steel/iron/Ni/Co by combustion & inert-gas fusion |
| **ASTM E1409** | **Oxygen and nitrogen** in titanium by inert-gas fusion |
| **ASTM E1447** | **Hydrogen** in titanium by inert-gas fusion |

→ [[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis]] · [[ONH inert gas fusion analysis]]

## How to use these
Pick the **method standard for your matrix** to govern the OES procedure; compare results to the
**material/product standard** for the grade ([[Standards overview]]). Calibrate with
matrix-matched CRMs traceable to the method ([[Calibration and certified reference materials]]).
