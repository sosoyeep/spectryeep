# ISO and EN methods

International (**ISO**) and European (**EN**, frequently identical to ISO) methods used alongside
or instead of ASTM, especially outside North America. **Verify current editions**
([[Standards overview]]).

## Spark / optical emission

- **EN 10184** — OES analysis of **low-alloy and unalloyed steel** (a European counterpart to
  [[ASTM methods for spark OES|ASTM E415]]).
- Various ISO/EN OES methods exist per matrix; product standards (EN 10025 steels, EN 573
  aluminium, EN 1982 copper castings, etc.) define the **composition limits** to compare
  against.
- **ISO 14707** — **GD-OES** (glow-discharge OES) — a *different* technique (depth profiling /
  surface), noted here to avoid confusion with spark OES.

## Combustion & fusion (C, S, O, N, H)

| Standard | Scope |
|---|---|
| **ISO 9556** | **Total carbon** in steel/iron by combustion + IR (→ [[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis]]) |
| **ISO 4935** | **Sulfur** in steel/iron by combustion + IR |
| **ISO 4829** | Silicon (chemical) — context for Si reference work |
| **ISO 15349** | Carbon/sulfur in unalloyed steel — IR after combustion (verify) |
| **ISO 10719 / others** | O/N determinations (verify designation per matrix) |
