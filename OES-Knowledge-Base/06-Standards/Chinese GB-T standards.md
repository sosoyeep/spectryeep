# Chinese GB / GB-T standards

China's national standards (**GB** = mandatory, **GB/T** = recommended). Directly relevant to
Chinese-built instruments such as the [[JIEBO product line]], and to customers certifying to
Chinese specs. **Verify current editions** ([[Standards overview]]).

## Spark OES test methods (by matrix)

| Standard | Scope (spark-OES analysis of …) | ~ASTM/ISO analogue |
|---|---|---|
| **GB/T 4336** | Carbon & low-alloy steel | [[ASTM methods for spark OES\|ASTM E415]] / EN 10184 |
| **GB/T 11170** | Stainless steel | ASTM E1086 |
| **GB/T 24234** | Cast iron | ASTM E1999 |
| **GB/T 7999** | Aluminium & aluminium alloys | ASTM E1251 |
| **GB/T 14203** | General principles of spark-OES (multi-element guidance) | — |

(Copper-alloy and nickel-alloy GB/T spark-OES methods also exist; verify the current designation
for your family.)

## Combustion / fusion (C, S, O, N, H)

| Standard | Scope |
|---|---|
| **GB/T 20123** | Total C & S in steel — high-frequency combustion + IR absorption |
| **GB/T 20124** | Nitrogen in steel — thermal-conductivity after fusion |
| **GB/T 11261** | Oxygen in steel — pulse-heating inert-gas fusion |
| **GB/T 223.x** | Classic chemical methods for elements in steel (reference series) |

→ [[Carbon-sulfur combustion analysis]] · [[ONH inert gas fusion analysis]]

## Product/material standards (composition limits)
GB/T 700 (carbon structural steel), GB/T 1591 (HSLA), GB/T 1220 (stainless bar), GB/T 9439 (grey
iron), GB/T 1348 (ductile iron), GB/T 1173 (cast aluminium) … — these define the **pass/fail**
composition you compare OES results against.
